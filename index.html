Everything You
Need to Know

How quickly can we expect to see results?
+
Paid advertising campaigns typically show measurable ROI within the first 30 days. SEO and content marketing deliver compounding results starting around months 3–6. Most clients see significant revenue impact within 90 days of full campaign launch.
What industries do you work with?
+
We have deep expertise in B2B SaaS, e-commerce, professional services, fintech, and enterprise software. Our team has managed campaigns in 30+ industries. We're particularly effective with companies that have a clear product-market fit and are ready to scale aggressively.
Do you require long-term contracts?
+
We work on rolling monthly agreements after an initial 3-month commitment period — enough time to properly execute strategy and demonstrate results. We don't lock you into long contracts because we believe results speak for themselves. Our 98% retention rate is proof.
How is performance tracked and reported?
+
Every client gets access to a real-time custom analytics dashboard showing all KPIs, ad performance, traffic metrics, lead generation, and revenue attribution. We provide weekly written reports, bi-weekly strategy calls, and 24/7 Slack access for enterprise clients.
What's your average ROAS across client accounts?
+
Our average ROAS is 6.3x across all active client accounts — with top performers achieving 9–12x. This is tracked across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn campaigns. We benchmark against industry averages monthly and optimize relentlessly until we're consistently outperforming them.
Do you work with companies outside the US?
+
Yes. While headquartered in New York, we manage campaigns for clients in 18 countries including the UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, and Singapore. Our team covers key time zones and we've built international audience frameworks for multilingual markets.
